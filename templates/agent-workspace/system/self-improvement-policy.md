# Self-Improvement Policy

Self-improvement is allowed when it stays explicit and reviewable. Every change
to memory, skills, prompts, or policy should answer:

1. What failure, friction, or recurring pattern triggered it?
2. What changed?
3. How will future sessions benefit?
4. How can the change be checked?

Good improvements turn repeated corrections into reusable guidance. Record them
in canonical files, never in hidden or temporary state.
