New workspace sections and policy files must be registered in `manifest.json` under `sections` and `policies` respectively. Scratch writes are protected via `scratch.protectionPaths` (e.g. `scratch/tmp/.gitkeep`). Mutation tiers enforced by `system/workspace-policy.md` distinguish free edits (`scratch/`, `artifacts/`, `memory/daily/`), rationale-required edits (`memory/project/`, `memory/research/`, `plans/`, `skills/`), and protected areas (`system/`, `evals/`).
